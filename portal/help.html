How to Use MACDEN Portal

A quick guide to every part of the portal.

Inbox & Messaging Staff Directory Leave Requests Documents & Policies Account Settings For Admins/HR

Inbox & Messaging

Click Compose in the sidebar to write a new message. Search for the person's name, click them to add, write a subject and message, then hit Send.

Your Inbox shows every message you've sent or received, newest first. Unread messages appear in bold. Click any row to open the full conversation.

Inside a conversation, use Reply or Reply All to respond — both currently send your reply to everyone in that conversation.

You can attach a PDF or Excel file to any message using the paperclip icon.

Staff Directory

Find anyone in the company under Directory. Search by name or username, and click a row to see their profile — role, department, email, and when they joined.

Click Message on someone's profile to start a conversation with them directly.

The green dot next to someone's name means they're currently active on the portal.

Requesting Leave

  1. Go to Leave & Requests
  2. Choose your leave type, start date, and end date
  3. Write a short reason
  4. Click Submit Request

You'll see your request under "My Requests" with a status: Pending, Approved, or Rejected. You'll also get a notification when it's reviewed.

Documents & Policies

Documents is a shared file library — HR forms, branch reports, templates. Use the categories on the left to filter, and click Download on any file.

Policies holds official company policies as readable pages — click Read Policy on any card to view the full text.

Account Settings

Under Settings, you can update your status/bio, choose which email notifications you receive, change your password, and sign out of every device at once if needed.

Your name, role, department, and join date are set by HR and can't be changed here — contact HR if any of that needs correcting.

For Admins & HR

If you're an admin, you'll see extra sidebar items: Broadcasts (send a message to everyone at once) and access to Manage Staff and Add New Staff from the Directory page.

New staff accounts you create will receive their login details by email automatically. If the email fails to send for any reason, the username and temporary password will be shown to you directly on screen instead — share those with the new staff member yourself in that case.

You're the only one who can approve leave requests and review the pending list under Leave & Requests.

Still stuck on something? Message the IT/Admin team directly through Compose.
